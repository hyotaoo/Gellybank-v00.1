Part A: Terms of Agreement

1. Acceptance
By ticking the agreement box and creating an account, you agree to these Terms with Gelly Bank ("Gelly Bank", "we", "us"). If you do not agree, do not create an account. These Terms apply to every Gelly Bank service you use, including the website, the customer dashboard and any related notification.

2. Who may open an account
To open an account you must:
• be at least 18 years old;
• provide true, complete and current personal information, and keep it updated;
• open and use the account only for yourself, never on behalf of someone else;
• complete any identity verification we require.
We may refuse an application, or ask for more information, without being obliged to give a reason beyond what is shown in your application status.

3. User roles
Gelly Bank has three kinds of users, each with limited powers.

Role: Customer
What they can do: View balances and history, request deposits and withdrawals, transfer funds, apply for more accounts, manage personal details and security
What they cannot do: Approve their own requests or see other customers' accounts

Role: Teller
What they can do: Look up customer accounts, verify identity and cash, approve or reject deposit and withdrawal requests, record the final status
What they cannot do: Change balances arbitrarily or change system settings

Role: Admin
What they can do: Create and deactivate Teller accounts, review and decide account applications, monitor transactions, view summary reports
What they cannot do: Process customer transactions, or silently edit or delete transaction amounts and records

4. Opening an account
You submit an application for a Savings or Checking account with the required information.
An Admin reviews it and either approves it, rejects it with a stated reason, or asks for more information.
The account becomes usable only after approval. You can track the status and read any rejection reason in your dashboard.
After approval you may apply for additional Savings or Checking accounts using the same process.
Only one person may use one account, and you may not sell, rent or transfer your account to anyone.

5. Deposits
Deposits are cash deposits confirmed by a Teller.
You submit a deposit request, choosing the receiving account and amount, with an optional note.
The request enters the Teller's queue as PENDING.
You present the cash to a Teller, who verifies the amount and the receiving account.
When the Teller confirms the deposit, the system credits the account and marks the transaction COMPLETED.
Your balance does not change until the Teller confirms. If the cash presented differs from the amount requested, or the request cannot be verified, the Teller may reject it and record the reason.

6. Withdrawals
You submit a withdrawal request for a chosen account and amount.
The system checks your available balance and applicable limits.
A Teller verifies your identity and the request, then approves or rejects it.
After approval, you receive the cash and the Teller confirms disbursement. Only then is the withdrawal recorded as COMPLETED.
A request that exceeds your available balance or limits, or whose identity check fails, will be rejected. You will see a reference number for every completed withdrawal.

7. Transfers
Transfers between accounts are electronic and do not wait for a Teller.
You enter the recipient's account number. The system shows the verified recipient name before you continue. Check it carefully.
You review the amount, any fee and the details, then confirm with your PIN or another authentication step.
The system validates the recipient, balance and limits, and then processes the transfer as one all-or-nothing operation. Both accounts and both transaction records are updated together, or neither is.
You receive a confirmation once the transfer is completed.
A confirmed transfer is final. Gelly Bank cannot recall funds sent to an account number you entered correctly. If you believe a transfer was unauthorised or sent in error, report it immediately under Section 12.

8. Transaction statuses and records
Every request carries one of four statuses.

PENDING: You submitted the request and Teller action is still needed
COMPLETED: The financial operation was successfully executed
REJECTED: The request was declined, and the reason is recorded
CANCELLED: The request was cancelled before it was executed

Each transaction receives a reference number. Our records, including the Teller who processed a transaction, are kept as the official record of your activity. Staff cannot silently edit an amount or delete a record. If you find an error, tell us and it will be corrected through a recorded entry.

9. Your security responsibilities
Keep your password and PIN secret. Never share them, even with someone who says they are from Gelly Bank. We will never ask for them.
Choose a strong password you do not use elsewhere, and change it if you suspect it is known to someone else.
Review your notifications and transaction history regularly.
Sign out after each session, especially on shared devices.
Tell us immediately if you notice a transaction you did not make, or if your credentials may be compromised.
Activity done with your credentials is treated as done by you, unless you reported the compromise before it happened or we were at fault.

10. AI Insights
AI Insights shows spending breakdowns, trends, budget suggestions, unusual-spending alerts and savings tips based on your own transaction records.
AI-generated suggestions are general information, not financial, tax or investment advice. Decide for yourself whether to act on them.
Your balances, statuses and transaction records are the verified banking information. Insights and categories may be estimated and can be wrong.
An unusual-spending alert is not proof of fraud. If you did not make a transaction, report it under Section 12.
How your data is used for this feature is explained in Part B.

11. Prohibited use

• give false identity details or open accounts under another person's identity;
• access or try to access another user's account, or any part of the system you are not permitted to use;
• interfere with the system, for example by scanning for weaknesses, overloading it, or bypassing limits and authentication;
• use bots, scripts or other tools to submit requests in a way that disrupts service;
• help anyone else do any of the above.

12. Reporting problems and disputes
Report unauthorised or mistaken transactions, lost credentials or suspected fraud as soon as you notice them, using the contact details in Section 18. Include the reference number, date and amount. We will acknowledge your report, investigate, and tell you the outcome and any correction made. Reports made promptly give us the best chance of limiting loss.

13. Fees and limits
Fees, if any, are shown before you confirm a transaction. Current fees: none for deposits, withdrawals, or transfers between Gelly Bank accounts.
Transaction limits apply: transfers of up to ₱50,000 each and ₱100,000 per day, and cash withdrawals of up to ₱50,000 per day. We may apply lower limits for security or compliance reasons.
₱100 minimum balance is required to open or keep a Savings or Checking account.
Interest on Savings accounts. Savings accounts earn interest at 3% per year. Interest is calculated each day on your ending balance, credited to your account once a month, and shown in your transaction history. Interest is reduced by taxes the law requires us to withhold (currently a 20% final withholding tax on peso deposit interest), so the amount credited is net of tax. Checking accounts do not earn interest. We may change the rate by giving you notice under Section 17.

Where the law allows, we will tell you why. You may ask to close your account at any time once your balance is withdrawn and no transaction is pending. Closing an account does not erase records we must keep by law (see Part B, Section 6).

Part B: Data Privacy Notice

1. Who we are and the law that applies
Gelly Bank is the personal information controller for the data described here. We handle your personal data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Because we are a bank, we also follow banking confidentiality, anti-money-laundering and financial consumer protection rules that apply to us.

Category — Examples — Where it comes from
Identity — Full name, date of birth, sex, nationality — You, at registration
Contact — Email address, mobile number, home address — You, at registration or profile updates
Verification — Type and number of a government-issued ID, verification status — You, and our staff who review your application

4. AI Insights and your data
AI Insights analyses your own transaction records inside Gelly Bank's systems. Your data is not sent to outside AI providers, and it is not used to build insights for other customers. The analysis produces spending categories, monthly summaries, trends, alerts and suggestions that appear only in your dashboard. Suggestions are labelled as AI-generated and kept apart from verified balances and records. It is a feature you choose: you may turn it off in your settings, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. Your banking services continue to work without it.

5. Who we share data with
We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for marketing by other companies. We disclose it only to:
Regulators and authorities, such as the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and the Anti-Money Laundering Council, or to law enforcement and courts, when the law or a valid order requires it.
Service providers who help us run the system, such as hosting and security services, under written agreements that limit use to our instructions and require them to protect the data. Current providers: a cloud hosting provider and an email and SMS notification provider.
The recipient of your transfer, who sees only your name and the details needed to complete the transfer.
Our own staff, strictly by role: Tellers see what is needed to process requests, and Admins see what is needed to approve accounts and monitor operations.
If we ever need to share your data in another way, we will ask for your consent first.

6. How long we keep data
Data — Kept for
Account and transaction records — At least five years from the transaction date or from account closure, as required by anti-money-laundering law, then securely deleted or anonymised
Identity and verification records — Same period as the account records
Rejected or withdrawn applications — One year after the decision, then deleted
Security and activity logs — One year
AI Insights results — Until you turn the feature off or close your account

Records we must keep by law are kept even if you ask us to delete them, and are used only for that legal purpose.

7. How we protect your data
Passwords and PINs are stored only in hashed form, never as readable text.
Data is encrypted while travelling between your device and our servers.
Access is role-based: Customers, Tellers and Admins can reach only what their role needs.
Money movements are processed as all-or-nothing operations, and transactions are recorded so they can be traced to the Teller or customer action behind them.
Staff accounts can be deactivated immediately.
If a breach puts your data at real risk of serious harm, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected within 72 hours of knowing about it, as the NPC requires.
No system is perfectly secure. Your own care with passwords and devices (Part A, Section 9) is part of keeping your data safe.
